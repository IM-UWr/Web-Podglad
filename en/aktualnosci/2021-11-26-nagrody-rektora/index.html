Rector's Awards

Achievement · Published 26 Nov 2021 · For: staff, students

The Rector of the University of Wrocław has granted awards to Institute staff for achievements in research, teaching and organisation.

On the occasion of the University Day, the Rector of the University of Wrocław granted awards to members of our Institute for achievements in organisation, teaching and research.

Research awards: Małgorzata Bogdan, Piotr Borodulin-Nadzieja, Dariusz Buraczewski, Krzysztof Dębicki, Piotr Dyszewski, Krzysztof Krupiński, Tomasz Rzepecki, Grzegorz Plebanek and Jacek Świątkowski.

Teaching awards: Tomasz Elsner and Jarosław Wróblewski; organisational award: Jan Kraszewski.
